XS drone · ladder frame

Frame jig, 1:1 on US Letter

Print the sheet at Actual size and check both 50 mm bars before cutting. Then lay the 1.0 mm brass rod straight onto the black lines and solder the joints in number order with rosin flux. The motor collars are wound from the 1.5 mm brass rod on a 7 mm former to fit the 7 × 20 mm coreless motors.

Lift revision, approved 2026-11-10: brass instead of stainless, axles 50 mm for the 40 mm props, collars 7 mm, skids 7 mm. The nose bar stays 31 mm (at 36 its ends hit the front collars) and the collar blanks are 66 mm, two turns plus a grip.
