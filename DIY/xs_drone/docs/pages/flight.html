Thirty-four seconds in the room

A scripted flight of the drone as drawn on 2026-11-10: arm, climb to the height the down-looking ranger holds, a full turn to look round, a run toward the far wall until the forward ranger says stop, the camera nodding down and up, a person crossing the room and the drone turning and following, then home and down. Left: the room from above with the rangers' fields and the camera's view. Right: the drone close up, with its instruments. The numbers come from the build page and the physics: nothing has flown yet, and this is a drawing that moves, not a simulation of the air.

What the flight shows, phase by phase

  • 0 to 2 s, armed on the floor. The flashlight blinks twice as the controller boots and the motion sensor settles. Nothing turns.
  • 2 to 5 s, spool and climb. The throttle rises to the 75 % cap and the drone lifts to 1.2 m, where the down-looking ranger takes over and holds the height. Climb current about 6.8 A, the most the pack is ever asked for.
  • 5 to 8 s, a turn on the spot. A full circle at 120° a second: the camera pans the whole room while the body stays level. Hover command about 64 %, about 5 A.
  • 8 to 14 s, toward the wall. The nose dips 8° and the drone crosses the room at half a metre a second. The forward 8×8 ranger watches the wall come in; at 0.8 m it says stop, and the drone holds there.
  • 14 to 18 s, the camera nods. The servo on the roof tilts the camera 30° down to look at the floor ahead, then back up. The body never tilts for it.
  • 18 to 26 s, following. A person crosses behind the drone. The desk computer finds them in the picture, the drone yaws to keep them centred and eases after them, holding about two metres; the side rangers keep the walls at bay.
  • 26 to 34 s, home and down. Back to the start, a slow descent on the down ranger, and the props stop. Thirty-four seconds of a flight the pack can sustain for about two and a quarter minutes.

The numbers behind the instruments

InstrumentHow it is computed here
Throttlethe hover command for the estimated 2.5 to one, about 64 %, plus 11 % to climb (capped at 75 %) and minus 6 % to descend; a small addition for forward speed
Currentfour 7 × 20 motors at 12 A full, scaled as the command squared: about 5 A in a hover, 6.8 A at the cap
Pack voltage3.7 V less the current through 70 mΩ of cell and pigtail, less a slow fall of 0.3 V over a full pack
Height, speed, yawfrom the scripted path, which stays inside what the thrust allows: 1.2 m hover, 0.5 m/s cruise, 120°/s yaw
Rangersstraight-line distances from the drone to the walls along the forward, left, right and rear fields; the forward one stops the run at 0.8 m
Camera tiltthe servo's lever: ±30° about the hinge pin at the cabin's front
