# Rev B parts list, 2026-10-31 (the order sheet)

Every part of Rev B is owned. Each row's Why cell opens with its status: **owned**, **owned, not used in Rev B** (held for
another project), **to buy** or **to print**. Prices are what was paid or read from the vendor page on the order date.
Supersedes the purchase tables in `BOM.md` and `bom/bom.csv`, which keep the September design reasoning. Each part is
named by what it is and linked to the exact product bought; the order-code column is there so a part can be re-bought.
The decision notes at the end are the history of this sheet (first verified 2026-10-19; Rev B 2026-10-31: solar removed,
compact body, 16340 cell). The page `pages/cyber-dawn-wiring.html` is built from the same list by
`pages/gen/wiring_outline.py`; edit the script, not this file.

## Decision notes

- **2026-10-19:** Core order verified against the vendor pages and placed at Adafruit, most lines ×2 for spares, $184.45 before shipping and tax. The mono speaker was out of stock, so the stereo pair. The solder-in proto board was dropped: the owned solderless breadboards carry Phase 1.
- **2026-10-26:** Battery and solar parts bought in one Adafruit order, $82.35 before shipping and tax: the 6600 mAh battery ($24.50, the builder's decision over the review's 4400 mAh pick, with the 6 h charge-timer limit recorded), the charger board ($8.95), the fuel gauge ($5.95), the panel-mount USB-C extension ($4.50), the 5 W solar panel ($34.95), the DC jack adapter ($1.50) and the DC jack to screw terminals ($2.00). The same day: the coin cell 10-pack, the 1200 mAh battery and the brass tube set from Amazon. Cheaper sources were checked first (the charger board at Micro Center Brooklyn, SKU 837740, $8.95 in-store; the fuel gauge at Mouser, 485-5580; generic 6 V panels at $12–18); the builder bought the Adafruit cart anyway. Next time, check the low-shipping sources first.
- **2026-10-28:** Controller board ordered from DigiKey, three pieces, with the drone's sensors. Correction: no controller was ordered on 2026-10-19 as this sheet had said.
- **2026-10-30:** Naming rule: component words with the link on the word; order codes only in the Order code column.
- **2026-10-31:** **Rev B: solar removed, compact body, 16340 cell.** The battery is one owned 16340 cell in the owned holder on an owned JST-PH pigtail, through the fuel gauge into the charger board's BAT port; charger jumper to 500 mA; charging through the charger board's own USB-C port. The 5 W solar panel, the DC jack adapter, the DC jack to screw terminals, the panel-mount USB-C extension, the 6600 mAh battery and the 1200 mAh battery are owned and held for another project. The body is 82 × 52 × 68 mm (COMPACT_DESIGN.md). Nothing to buy but the acrylic face and the PETG print.
- **2026-11-05:** **Sunrise lamp added** at the builder's request: an owned LED filament in the lid, fed from the cell + through 15 Ω (not the 1 A boost, which an alarm already nearly fills), switched by an owned MOSFET on GPIO40, faded up over 30 minutes by the clock's Alarm 2. Nothing to buy.

**Spent so far:** $184.45 at Adafruit on 2026-10-19, $45.00 at DigiKey on 2026-10-28 for the controllers, $82.35 at Adafruit on 2026-10-26 for the battery and solar parts, plus the Amazon lines with prices not recorded; all before shipping and tax. Of the $82.35, $67.45 is now held stock (panel, adapters, extension, 6600 mAh battery) and $14.90 (charger board, fuel gauge) is in Rev B.
