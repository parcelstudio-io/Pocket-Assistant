# Purchased inventory — Cyber Dawn

Factual purchase record. What was *bought*, not what is approved; part authority stays in
[`REVIEW.md`](REVIEW.md) and the order list in [`ORDER_SHEET.md`](ORDER_SHEET.md). Everything owned
from the Pocket AI build (amplifier, switch, passives, wire, tools) is in
`../../pocket_ai_assistant/docs/INVENTORY.md` and is not repeated here.

**Rev B 2026-10-31: solar removed, compact body, 16340 cell.** The battery is one Nitecore 16340 cell in the owned [16340 holder with leads](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CRGK889F) on an owned JST-PH pigtail, through the [fuel gauge](https://www.adafruit.com/product/5580) into the [charger board](https://www.adafruit.com/product/6106)'s BAT port, jumper at 500 mA; the charger board's own USB-C port charges it through the rear panel. The 5 W [solar panel](https://www.adafruit.com/product/5367), the [DC jack adapter](https://www.adafruit.com/product/4287), the [DC jack to screw terminals](https://www.adafruit.com/product/368), the [panel-mount USB-C extension](https://www.adafruit.com/product/6069), the 6600 mAh [battery](https://www.adafruit.com/product/353) and the 1200 mAh [battery](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09G2S88Q3) stay listed below as owned, held for another project.

In Rev B (2026-10-31) the frame is two bent brass U cradles under the 82 × 52 × 68 mm body, about 150 mm of 3 mm tube each ([`COMPACT_DESIGN.md`](COMPACT_DESIGN.md)); the exoskeleton and the snooze rail are retired. The 3 mm tube is the one the design wants (`BOM.md` called for 3 mm rod or 1/8" tube; 1/8" is 3.175 mm, so 3 mm is the closest metric match); 2 mm matches the reference clock's thinner lines; 4–7 mm are for the stand feet and a sleeve test only. One tube per size: mock up in the owned 1.5 mm K&S stock first and cut the METERXITY only for the final frame. Brass stays electrically floating per `hardware/README.md`. |
