Where the charger board's LEDs are

Drawn the way your photo shows the board: USB-C port at the top, the eight edge pads down the left, the white battery socket on the right, the green screw block at the bottom.

Orange, marked C: chargingRight of the USB-C port, the right-hand one of the two small LEDs, under the letter C. On while the cell charges. Off when full, or when it can't see the cell.
Red, marked F: faultJust left of the orange one. Red alone clears itself. Red and orange together is a latched fault: unplug USB-C and plug it back in.
Green: 5 V output liveNear the green screw block and its 5V label. Lights from the cell alone, even with USB-C out. Its exact spot is drawn from Adafruit's description, not your photo.

The LEDs are tiny surface parts, about the size of a grain of rice. Look for the glow, not the part.
