What the clock will look like

The clock is now a block of 82 × 52 × 68 mm, half the width of the first drawing, with the owned 2.42 inch OLED as most of its face. A warm LED filament in the lid fades up before the alarm, like a sunrise. It charges over USB-C and carries one 16340 cell as a backup; the solar panel is gone from the design and stays in the drawer for another project. Every part is drawn at its datasheet size, so what fits here fits on the bench. Nothing is built yet. Wiring is not drawn; that lives on the wiring sheet, and the reasoning is in COMPACT_DESIGN.md.
