The bridge

How the MAX98357A drives the speaker: four transistor switches in an H, the speaker as the crossbar, and only two legal ways to close them. Four drawings.

In one breath

Two legs, four switches, diagonal pairs only. State 1 pushes the cone out, state 2 pulls it in, and the share of time in each is the sound. Both OUT pins are driven midpoints, so tying either to a fixed voltage forces a leg into the forbidden state. The speaker is the only thing that may sit between them.
