Pip as rails and lanes

No positions, only connections. Three rails along the top carry power: 5 V from the PowerBoost, 3V3 from A1's regulator, GND. Nine lanes along the bottom carry signals, one each. Every part hangs between the rails and the lanes; a pin that touches a rail or a lane is a joint. Place the parts anywhere; keep the joints.

How to use this for a layout you choose yourself

  1. Make the three rails physical: three bare rods, any length you like, anywhere you like, 3 mm apart from each other and from everything else. Every GND goes to the GND rod, every VDD/VCC to the 3V3 rod, VIN and A1's 5V to the 5 V rod.
  2. Then each signal lane is one piece of wire or rod from its A1 pin to the pin that uses it; WS and BCLK pass the amp (soldered) on their way to the mic.
  3. The three rules do not move: OUT+ and OUT− only to the speaker; the frame touches nothing; one power source at a time (the PowerBoost on, A1's port unused).
  4. Everything else about distances and heights on the harness page is one worked answer to this diagram, not the only one.
