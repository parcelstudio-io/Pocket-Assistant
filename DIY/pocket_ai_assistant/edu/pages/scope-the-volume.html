Pocket AI Assistant · Step 11 · the scope has arrived

Measure the speaker, then raise the volume

A1 now plays at volume cap 30 with the amplifier's GAIN at 15 dB, its highest setting, and it is still quiet. Cap 30 was allowed without the scope; going past it needs a measurement. Measure what A1 really puts across the speaker at cap 30, calculate the cap that stays under the speaker's 2.53 V limit, and prove that cap on the dummy load before the speaker hears it.

Up to cap 30

Allowed now, no scope needed. The datasheet worst case is 0.98 V peak, under the 2.53 V limit.

Above cap 30

Needs the dummy load and PASS 11B at that cap: the loudest filtered peak at or below 2.53 V.

Why 2.53 V

√(0.8 W × 8 Ω) = 2.53 V. A peak at or below it keeps even a clipped, square-ish signal within the speaker's 0.8 W.

Rules for the whole session
  • No ground clip ever goes on OUT+ or OUT−. Both ground clips go on A1's GND. The outputs are not ground; a clip there shorts the amplifier through the scope.
  • Unplug USB before every probe or wiring change.
  • An image with a cap above 30 only ever runs into the dummy load until PASS 11B passes at that cap.
  • USB stays the only power. The scope's own adapter powers only the scope.

0:00Check the scope before it touches A1

  1. Unbox and inspect: the DHO802, its USB-C 15 V adapter, the banana ground cable, and two PVP3150 probes. Power it up on the bench, nothing connected.

  2. Run self-calibration from the Utility menu while it warms. It takes several minutes; leave the inputs empty.

  3. Compensate each probe. Set the probe switch and the channel to 10X. Hook the tip to the scope's compensation output and the clip to its ground tab. Turn the probe's trimmer until the 1 kHz square wave has flat tops, not rounded or spiked corners.

  4. Find out whether the grounds are earthed. Meter on continuity, one probe on a BNC input's metal shell, the other on the adapter's earth pin. Write down beep or no beep.

0:15Hook up, USB unplugged

The speaker can stay on for this measurement, because A1 runs cap 30, which your decision of 22 September allows directly. You measure the voltage between the two outputs, so each probe goes to one output and both grounds go to A1's GND.

0:20Set the scope, then read three long replies

The amplifier drives its outputs as fast on-off pulses, each swinging the full 5 V, and lets the speaker's coil smooth them into sound. The scope sees the raw pulses, so an unfiltered reading always says about 5 V, whatever the volume. A low-pass filter on the math trace removes the pulses and leaves the audio you want to measure.

  1. Plug USB in and wait for A1 to reach idle.

  2. Reset the measurement statistics, quick-press the button and ask for something long, such as "Tell me a story about a lighthouse".

  3. Do it three times without resetting the statistics. The Max of Vmax after the third reply is V₃₀. Write it down and photograph the screen.

0:35Turn V₃₀ into a cap

The firmware squares the volume before it scales the audio, so the peak grows with the square of the cap: V(cap) = V₃₀ × (cap ÷ 30)². Going from cap 30 to 45 makes the peak 2.25 times larger. The calculator keeps a 20 % margin under 2.53 V, because a louder reply than the three you measured is always possible.

Example value shown. Type your reading.

0:40Build the louder image

Choose the cap from the calculator. If it says 30, stop after this section: cap 30 needs no dummy load, so put the speaker back and skip to the last section.

  1. Change one line in firmware/sdkconfig.defaults, which now reads …=30, putting your cap in place of 45. Or ask Claude to do the rebuild.

    CONFIG_POCKET_AI_MAX_VOLUME=45

  2. Rebuild and verify. About five minutes. Continue only if the verifier prints amplifier: ENABLED (Step 11, USB-powered).

    . firmware/.work/esp-idf/export.sh
firmware/scripts/build.sh --assistant --amplifier
python3 firmware/scripts/verify_source_build.py

  3. Above 30: swap in the dummy load first. USB unplugged, speaker leads out of the terminal block, the dummy load's two wires in. The new image must never play into the speaker before PASS 11B.

  4. Flash, keeping Wi-Fi, and find Prototype volume cap: 45 in the log.

    firmware/scripts/flash.sh /dev/ttyACM0 --keep-wifi --monitor

  5. No voice command needed. Since 28 September A1 starts every boot at its cap, so the log also shows start volume: 45.

0:50PASS 11B on the dummy load

Same probes, same settings, same three long replies, now into the dummy load at the new cap.

0:55Speaker back on

  1. Unplug USB, take the scope probes off, swap the dummy load for the speaker, and tighten both screws until a gentle tug does not move the leads.

  2. Five long replies in a row. Stop and unplug if you hear harsh buzzing or smell anything, or if the speaker gets warm.
