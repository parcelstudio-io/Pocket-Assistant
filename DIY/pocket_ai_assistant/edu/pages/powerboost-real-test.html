A1 on the PowerBoost: the real test

Eight steps, one rule. Needs the Adafruit PowerBoost 1000C, the 1200 mAh cell, two short wires, and the server running on the computer. The result shows up in the server log over Wi-Fi, so A1 needs no USB cable at all.

The one rule

A1's USB cable and the PowerBoost output are never connected at the same time. Switch the PowerBoost off before plugging A1 in; unplug A1 before switching on.

If step 4 gives no eyes: meter 5V to GND on the PowerBoost with the switch on (5.1–5.3 V); then the two wires at A1's stubs. If the log shows got ip but no hello, it is the server, not the power.
