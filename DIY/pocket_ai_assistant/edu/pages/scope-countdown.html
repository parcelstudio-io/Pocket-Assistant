Nine days to the scope: learn two hours, build one

Every day has the same shape: two illustrated lessons in two hours, then one hour at the bench moving the device forward. The build hours finish Step 11 early: A1 speaks through its speaker on Wednesday 23, and the scope, arriving Wednesday 30 September, then measures what already works.

Rules for every day
  • The cells and charger stay terminal-protected in another room. This is a USB-powered device; they are not part of it.
  • Unplug USB before every wiring change. The amplifier connects only as Step 11 describes, powered from the controller's 5V pin.
  • Neither speaker lead is ground, and no probe ground ever goes on one.
  • If you open the bench supply, it is 3.3 V with a written current limit and the six-move sequence at the bottom of this page.
  • Stop at three hours. One study log per day; one lab record per build hour.

Build · 1 hour · the amplifier and speaker, unpowered

  1. Nothing is powered: bench supply unplugged, no USB in any module, cells and charger in another room.

  2. Meter check: red lead in V/Ω, short the probes, and write down the lead resistance. At 8 Ω a 0.2 Ω offset matters.

  3. Label one HiLetgo board AMP-A1 and leave the other two bagged. Label the speaker SPK-A1. Photograph both sides at phone macro zoom.

  4. Trace the amplifier's silkscreen: VIN, GND, BCLK, LRC, DIN, GAIN, SD and the two speaker terminals. Draw every resistor you can see around SD and note any value code printed on it. Step 11 picks its pull-down from this drawing.

  5. Continuity, unpowered: each speaker terminal against the amplifier's GND pad. Then the speaker's DC resistance across its leads, minus the lead offset, labelled resistance, not impedance.

  6. Draw the rule sheet: the floating bridge-tied connection and three forbidden ones, OUT+ to GND, OUT− to GND, and a scope ground on either lead. Tape it above the bench.

If you already did this lab on Monday, use the hour for Wednesday's build instead. The Lesson 10 page explains every step; its reading comes on Sunday.
End of the day

Build · 1 hour · build the dummy load, for louder settings on the 30th

  1. Measure a few of the kit's 100 Ω resistors to confirm the value. If the kit has none, any value from 68 Ω to 120 Ω works: use the value divided by eight of them.

  2. Lay two short 26 AWG wires side by side and solder twelve resistors across them like the rungs of a ladder, each leg to both wires.

  3. Measure the ladder, subtract the lead offset, and label it with the reading, about 8.3 Ω.

Build · 1 hour · I2S-01 and SD-RESET-01 on A1

  1. A1 runs the speaking image from Wednesday 23, amplifier enabled at cap 15. Channel 1 on GPIO1, channel 2 on GPIO2, both ground clips on GND. Read both frequencies: 16.000 kHz and 1.024 MHz within 0.5 % closes gate 4 on evidence. Save screenshots.

  2. Move channel 1 to the amplifier's SD pin. Single trigger, rising edge at 0.08 V. Unplug USB, then plug it back in while holding A1's BOOT button, five times. BOOT keeps the firmware from starting, so only the pull-down holds SD. If the trigger never fires, SD stayed in shutdown through every power-up. Save the result either way.
