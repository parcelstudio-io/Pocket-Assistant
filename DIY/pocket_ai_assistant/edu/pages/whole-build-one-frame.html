The whole build in one frame

Harness, PowerBoost, cell and switch, all inside one 68 × 42 × 38 frame, drawn storey by storey from the same model. Every rod of the harness is here unchanged (its own page has the per-rod drawings and cut lengths); this page adds the five connections that make it a battery device and shows where each thing physically sits.

Build order for the one-frame version

  1. The harness, moves 1–11 of the harness page, on USB, on its jig card. Make it work first.
  2. The frame to the new size: two loops at 65 × 39 inside, four posts of 35. Kapton inside.
  3. The shelf: a 64 × 38 card with the two wire holes and the LBO hole punched where the storey-2 drawing shows; the switch glued at its front edge.
  4. PowerBoost on the shelf, USB port at the right wall; wires P1–P4 (and P6) soldered at the PowerBoost end first, led up through the holes.
  5. Harness off its jig and onto the shelf; P1 to A1's 5V stub, P2 to A1's GND stub. Kapton over A1's USB.
  6. Cradle and cell at the bottom, JST into the PowerBoost. Meter frame to everything: silent.
  7. Switch on: the same tests as move 11. Then charge it once and watch the LEDs.

Sources. The harness from edu/pages/gen/harness_model.py; PowerBoost 1000C dimensions, pins and functions from Adafruit's product page; cell size from docs/INVENTORY.md. The lower storeys are a layout proposal measured against those sizes, not a built thing; pin positions on the PowerBoost are drawn as a group and must be read off its silkscreen.
