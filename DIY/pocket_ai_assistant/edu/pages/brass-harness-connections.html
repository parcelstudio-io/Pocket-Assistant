Brass harness: what connects to what

The whole harness as one list. 11 pins leave A1; 2 rails carry power and ground to everything; 2 resistors and 2 capacitors sit between. Rod names, lengths and heights match the step-by-step page, which is where the drawings and the build order are.

The two rails

A rail is a bare, straight 1 mm rod lying on the jig card during the build; in the finished device it hangs on its taps, lowest thing in the cage. It is the one wire that many parts share: every part's ground hooks onto the GND rail, every 3.3 V part hooks onto the 3V3 rail. A1 feeds each rail once; after that nobody runs a rod back to A1 for power.

Never connected

  • OUT+ and OUT− go only to the speaker's own two leads. Never to GND, a rail, or the frame.
  • The frame carries nothing. Every rail and rod stays 2 mm from it, with Kapton on the tubes.
  • 5 V reaches the amp only. The screen and mic live on 3V3.
