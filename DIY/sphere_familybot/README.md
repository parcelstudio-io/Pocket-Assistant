# Sphere FamilyBot

A fat, cute sphere robot like the Enabot EBO Max, driven from a phone: a Ø200 mm white sphere on hidden wheels with one 5 inch
round screen for a face, live video, talk, patrols, a block editor for kids and a voice with the desktop's language
model behind it. One stainless line, the family's, on a white body; prototype not product.

**Status 2026-11-03: feasibility study only.** Nothing bought, printed, soldered or flashed; no dimensioned form drawing,
wiring sheet, edu page or firmware yet, only a shaded view of how it looks inside the study. The house rule's step one (the final build diagram, stopped for
approval) comes after the study is read.

- [docs/FEASIBILITY.md](docs/FEASIBILITY.md): what the EBO Max actually is, the three sphere geometries and the
  pick, the Raspberry Pi 5 versus Jetson decision, the architecture, each of the eight use cases with an honest
  verdict, parts with prices read today, size, mass, runtime, the design direction, a three-stage plan and the
  open questions.
- [docs/ARTIFACTS.md](docs/ARTIFACTS.md): the published page.
- `docs/pages/gen/feasibility_page.py` writes the page and the Letter PDF from the markdown; edit the markdown,
  rerun the script.
